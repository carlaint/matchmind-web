Questions

 

Where are my notes stored?

On your iPhone. If iCloud is on, Apple also backs them up to your own private iCloud, so they appear on your other devices. They're never stored on our servers.

Can you read my notes?

No. We never store them. When you ask for AI insights, your notes pass through our server to the AI and aren't kept. See our privacy policy for the details.

What happens if I lose or replace my phone?

With iCloud backup on, sign in on your new phone and your notes come back. If you don't use iCloud, export a backup from the Account tab to keep a copy.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Recording, typing and browsing your notes all work offline. AI insights and signing in need a connection.

Which sports does it support?

MatchMind is built for tennis today. Pickleball and padel are coming.

When can I get it?

MatchMind is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.
