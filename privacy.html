Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Your notes live on your phone. We never see or store them. They're only sent to our AI to create your insights. Only notes you choose to share with your team (Captain plan) are stored, and they're deleted when the season ends.

The short version

  • Your notes, players and match details are saved on your iPhone, and backed up to your own private iCloud if you use iCloud.
  • Our server stores only your account and how many AI insights you've used.
  • When you ask for AI insights, your notes pass through our server to our AI provider. We don't keep them.
  • No ads, no trackers, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your notes and voice note text
  • Players you track (names and ratings)
  • Match details: dates, scores, results, partners, court, surface, time of day and weather
  • Saved courts
  • AI insights and your strategy feedback

iCloud backup

If iCloud is on for MatchMind, Apple backs up this data to your private iCloud storage and syncs it to your other devices. It's stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, and we can't see it. You can turn this off in iOS Settings. If iCloud is off, your notes exist only on your phone, so export a copy from the Account tab.

What our server stores

  • Your name, email address and username
  • Your password, scrambled with bcrypt (we can't see it)
  • Your preferred format (singles or doubles)
  • Your plan and how many AI insights you've used this period
  • Login sessions
  • If you use Sign in with Apple: an ID code from Apple, and the email Apple shares with us. That can be a private relay address that hides your real email. We never receive your Apple ID password or anything in your iCloud.
  • If you subscribe: payment is handled by Apple. We receive the plan, its dates and a transaction ID from Apple, never your payment details.

Our server is hosted by Render. It doesn't store your notes, players, match details or AI insights.

AI insights

When you ask for insights about a player (or the app refreshes them after you save a note), the app sends that player's notes and match details over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude AI and returns the result. Our server doesn't save or log your notes or the insights; the insights are saved on your phone.

Under its commercial terms, Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models and automatically deletes it within 30 days, unless it's flagged for violating Anthropic's usage policy or must be kept by law.

Voice notes

Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio never leaves your phone and is never recorded or stored. Only the text is saved, on your phone.

Court locations and weather

When you search for a court, what you type is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places. When you save a note with a court, the court's map location and the match date are sent to Apple Weather to get that day's conditions. MatchMind never asks for or uses your own location.

Team sharing (Captain plan)

If you use team features, you choose which opponent notes to share. Shared notes are stored on our server for your team only, and are deleted when the season ends, when you unshare them, or when you leave the team. Partner notes can't be shared. Match results (scores, partners and wins or losses, not notes) are shared with your team automatically.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI. We don't use advertising networks, analytics, trackers or social media SDKs.

Your choices

  • Export your notes any time, and import a backup (Account tab)
  • Edit your profile and password (Account tab)
  • Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete Account). This removes your account from our server right away, and you can also delete your notes from your phone and iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes notes from your phone. Copies in iCloud stay until you delete them (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage).

Security

All connections use HTTPS encryption. Passwords are scrambled with bcrypt, and your login is kept in the iPhone's secure Keychain.

Services we use

  • Anthropic (Claude): creates AI insights from your notes
  • Render: hosts our server and account database
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, iCloud backup, on-device speech recognition, Maps search and Weather

Children

MatchMind isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

AI-generated content

AI insights are informational and may contain mistakes. Use your own judgment on court.

Changes and contact

If we make significant changes to this policy, we'll let you know in the app. Questions? Email privacy@matchmindai.app
